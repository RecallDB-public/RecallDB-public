FSIS recalls and public health alerts for meat, poultry and processed egg products. RecallDB normalizes the USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service feed into the same schema as the other four agencies, keeping every row's source_url and a source_id that resolves to data_sources.csv.

The free sample holds 40 FSIS rows. Their leading hazard buckets are microbial contamination (28), undeclared allergen (11), other / unmapped (1); severity labels present: High - Class I (22), Public Health Alert (17), Low - Class II (1).

Source endpoint: https://www.fsis.usda.gov/fsis/api/recall/v/1, retrieved 2026-07-13 (11,570,810 bytes raw).
